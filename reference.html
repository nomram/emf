Key formulas by chapter

I · Fields

  • 1. $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$ (long wire); $B_z=\dfrac{\mu_0Ia^2}{2(a^2+z^2)^{3/2}}$ (loop axis); Larmor $P=\dfrac{q^2a^2}{6\pi\varepsilon_0c^3}$.
  • 2. $\vec F=q(\vec E+\vec v\times\vec B)$; $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}$, $f_c=\dfrac{|q|B}{2\pi m}$; $\vec D=\varepsilon\vec E$, $\vec B=\mu\vec H$; $u=\tfrac12\varepsilon E^2+\tfrac12\mu H^2$.
  • 3. $\mathcal E=-N\,d\Phi/dt$; $c=1/\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}$; $E/H=\eta_0$; $S=E^2/\eta_0$.
  • 4. $S=\dfrac{P_tG}{4\pi r^2}$; $E_{rms}=\dfrac{\sqrt{30P_tG}}{r}$; near-field boundary $\lambda/2\pi$; far field $2D^2/\lambda$.

II · Spectrum & matter

  • 5. $c=f\lambda$; $E=hf$; $\lambda_{peak}=2898\ \mu\text{m K}/T$.
  • 5a. $E_n=-13.6\ \text{eV}/n^2$; $KE=E_\gamma-E_{bind}$; $I=I_0e^{-\mu x}$.
  • 5b. $y_m=m\lambda L/d$; $\lambda_{dB}=h/p$; $V^2+K^2\le1$.
  • 6. skin depth $\delta=1/\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}$; $\tan\delta=\sigma/\omega\varepsilon$; loss $=8.686\,x/\delta$ dB.
  • 7. $L_p=20\log_{10}(p/20\,\mu\text{Pa})$; $v=\sqrt{B/\rho}$.
  • 8. seawater $\delta\approx250/\sqrt f$ m; $R_{rad}=20\pi^2(L/\lambda)^2$; $C=B\log_2(1+S/N)$.

III · Antennas

  • 9. half-wave dipole $73\ \Omega$, $D=1.64$ (2.15 dBi), $L\approx143/f_\text{MHz}$ m; $D=4\pi U_{max}/P_{rad}$; $A_e=G\lambda^2/4\pi$; Friis $P_r=P_tG_tG_r\big(\tfrac{\lambda}{4\pi d}\big)^2$; FSPL $=20\log d+20\log f-147.55$.
  • 10. $\Gamma=\dfrac{Z_L-Z_0}{Z_L+Z_0}$; VSWR $=\dfrac{1+|\Gamma|}{1-|\Gamma|}$; RL $=-20\log|\Gamma|$; quarter-wave transformer $Z_T=\sqrt{Z_0Z_L}$; L-match $Q=\sqrt{R_{hi}/R_{lo}-1}$.
  • 11. HPBW$\approx70\lambda/D$°; dish $G=\eta(\pi D/\lambda)^2$; horn $G=\eta4\pi A/\lambda^2$; Ruze loss $e^{-(4\pi\varepsilon/\lambda)^2}$.
  • 11a. radio horizon $d\approx4.12(\sqrt{h_1}+\sqrt{h_2})$ km; plasma $f_p=8.98\sqrt{N_e}$ Hz; MUF$=f_c\sec\varphi$; Carson $BW=2(\Delta f+f_m)$.

IV · Radar & resilience

  • 12. $R=ct/2$; $\Delta R=c\tau/2$; $R_u=c/2\text{PRF}$; $P_r=\dfrac{P_tG^2\lambda^2\sigma}{(4\pi)^3R^4}$; $N=kT_0BF$.
  • 13. $f_d=2v_r/\lambda$; $v_u=\lambda\text{PRF}/4$; $R_uv_u=c\lambda/8$; FMCW $f_b=2RB/(cT_c)$, $\Delta R=c/2B$.
  • 14. SAR cross-range $\approx D/2$; $L_{syn}=R\lambda/D$; rain $\gamma=kR^\alpha$ dB/km.
  • 15. $\sigma_{sphere}=\pi r^2$ (optical), $9\pi r^2x^4$ (Rayleigh); plate $4\pi A^2/\lambda^2$; corner $4\pi a^4/3\lambda^2$; range $\propto\sigma^{1/4}$.
  • 15a. processing gain $G_p=R_{chip}/R_{bit}$; MVDR $\mathbf w=\mathbf R^{-1}\mathbf a/(\mathbf a^H\mathbf R^{-1}\mathbf a)$.
  • 15b. detection range $R_{max}=\left[\dfrac{P_tG^2\lambda^2\sigma}{(4\pi)^3kT_0BF\,\text{SNR}}\right]^{1/4}$ (range $\propto\sigma^{1/4}$); weather $\eta=\dfrac{\pi^5}{\lambda^4}|K|^2Z$, $Z=\sum D^6$ (dBZ).
  • 15c. bistatic $P_r=\dfrac{P_tG_tG_r\lambda^2\sigma_b}{(4\pi)^3R_t^2R_r^2}$; forward scatter $\sigma_{fs}=4\pi A^2/\lambda^2$; processing gain $\approx B\,T$.
  • 15d. echo phase $\Delta\varphi=4\pi x/\lambda$; micro-Doppler $f_d=2v/\lambda$; power density $S=P/4\pi r^2$.

V · EMP

  • 16. $E(t)=E_0k(e^{-\alpha t}-e^{-\beta t})$; bandwidth $\approx1/t_{rise}$; wire resonances $f_n=nc/2L$; SE $\approx20\log(\lambda/2l)$ for a slot, absorption $8.686\,t/\delta$.
  • 16a. below-cutoff attenuation ≈ 32 dB per diameter (round) or 27.3 dB per width (rectangular); cutoff circular $f_c=0.586\,c/D$, rectangular $f_c=c/2a$; shield absorption $8.686\,t/\delta$ dB; slot leakage $20\log(\lambda/2l)$; transfer impedance $V=Z_tI_s$ with a pigtail $Z\approx j\omega L_p$ (≈ 10 nH/cm).
  • 16b. light travel 1 AU $=499$ s; geomagnetically induced current $I=E\,L/R$; dipole field $B_{eq}=31.2\,\mu\text{T}/L^3$, L-shell $r=L\cos^2\lambda$; gyro-frequency $f_g=qB/2\pi m$; loss cone $\sin^2\alpha_{LC}=B_{eq}/B_{mirror}$.
  • 17. dielectric heating $p=2\pi f\varepsilon_0\varepsilon''E^2$; cavity modes $f=\tfrac c2\sqrt{(m/a)^2+(n/b)^2+(p/d)^2}$; Hull cutoff $B_c=\dfrac{\sqrt{8mV/e}}{b(1-a^2/b^2)}$.

VI · Arrays

  • 18. $AF=\sum w_ne^{jnkd(\sin\theta-\sin\theta_0)}$; $d\le\lambda/(1+|\sin\theta_{max}|)$; beamwidth $0.886\lambda/(Nd\cos\theta_0)$; scan loss $\cos\theta_0$.
  • 19. virtual array $N\times M$; interferometer $\theta\approx\lambda/B_{max}$.

Glossary

AESA
Active electronically scanned array: a transmit/receive module behind every element.
Aperture
The effective area that collects or radiates a wave; sets beamwidth and gain.
Array factor
The pattern of a set of identical antennas, ignoring the element’s own pattern.
Balun
Balanced-to-unbalanced transformer.
Beamwidth (HPBW)
Angle between half-power (−3 dB) points of the main lobe.
Chirp
A signal whose frequency changes steadily with time.
Clutter
Unwanted radar echoes from ground, sea, rain, birds.
Decoherence
Loss of quantum interference through interaction with the environment.
Directivity / gain
Concentration of radiated power in a direction vs an isotropic radiator; gain includes efficiency.
Doppler shift
Change in frequency from relative motion.
EIRP
Transmit power times antenna gain.
EMP
Electromagnetic pulse: a brief, broadband, intense transient.
FMCW
Frequency-modulated continuous-wave radar.
Frequency hopping
Jumping the carrier through a pseudo-random sequence of channels.
Bistatic radar
Radar whose transmitter and receiver are at different places; passive versions use transmitters of opportunity.
CME
Coronal mass ejection: a cloud of magnetised plasma thrown out by the Sun, arriving in about 1–3 days.
dBZ
Logarithmic unit of weather-radar reflectivity factor Z.
Dst index
Measure of the ring-current disturbance of Earth’s magnetic field during a geomagnetic storm, in nanotesla.
GIC
Geomagnetically induced current: quasi-DC current driven in power lines and pipelines by a storm.
Grating lobe
An extra main-beam-like lobe from array spacing larger than λ/2.
Hull cutoff
Magnetic field above which electrons in a magnetron cannot reach the anode.
L-shell
Label of the magnetic field line crossing the equator at L Earth radii; organises the Van Allen belts.
I/Q
In-phase and quadrature components of a signal; a complex number per sample.
Ionisation
Removal of an electron from an atom or molecule.
Ionosphere
Layer of the upper atmosphere with free electrons that refracts HF radio waves.
Interferometer
An array whose element pairs (baselines) measure Fourier components of the sky.
LPI
Low probability of intercept: designed to be hard to detect.
MIMO
Multiple-input multiple-output: several transmit and receive antennas.
MUF
Maximum usable frequency for skywave reflection.
Micro-Doppler
Small Doppler modulations from moving parts of a target such as limbs, rotor blades or a chest.
Null
Direction of zero response in a pattern.
PRF / PRI
Pulse repetition frequency / interval.
Polarisation
Direction of the electric field of a wave.
Processing gain
Improvement in signal-to-interference from spreading, $R_{chip}/R_{bit}$.
RCS (σ)
Radar cross section in m²: how strongly a target reflects.
SAA
South Atlantic Anomaly: the region where the inner Van Allen belt dips closest to Earth.
SAR
Synthetic aperture radar.
SDR
Software-defined radio: digitise early, process in software.
Solar flare
Sudden release of magnetic energy on the Sun; classes A, B, C, M, X by peak soft X-ray flux.
Skin depth
Depth at which a wave in a conductor falls to 1/e.
SOFAR channel
Deep ocean sound channel that traps low-frequency sound.
Spread spectrum
Using far more bandwidth than the data needs, for robustness or secrecy.
Standing wave
Fixed pattern formed by forward and reflected waves; VSWR measures its contrast.
Van Allen belts
Doughnut-shaped regions of charged particles trapped by Earth’s magnetic field: inner (protons) and outer (electrons).
VSWR
Voltage standing-wave ratio.
Waveguide (below cutoff)
Hollow tube carrying a wave above its cutoff frequency; below cutoff it blocks waves while letting fibres or air through.
Feedthrough capacitor
A capacitor mounted through a shield wall, with almost no lead inductance, used to filter lines at the entry point.
Transfer impedance
Ratio of the voltage induced on a cable’s inner conductor to the shield current, a measure of a cable shield’s quality.
Wavefunction
Complex amplitude whose squared magnitude gives probability.
