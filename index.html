The whole idea in one sentence

A changing electric field makes a magnetic field, a changing magnetic field makes an electric field, and the two can hold each other up and leave the wire as a wave at the speed of light.

Everything in this guide is that sentence, followed by “and what happens if I shape it, tune it, bounce it, or steer it?”. Orange always means the electric field E, cyan always means the magnetic field B or H, and yellow means energy or power.
